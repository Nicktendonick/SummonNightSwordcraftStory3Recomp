# Launcher artwork provenance

These are user-supplied images for the local beta launcher, copied without
retouching, cropping, resampling, or stripping embedded credits.
No redistribution license or ownership is inferred from their submission.
Confirm distribution rights before packaging a public release.

The nine `art-*.png` files correspond to the nine images supplied on
2026-09-26, in order:

1. `art-archer.png` — pink archer poster
2. `art-winter.png` — winter duo / village
3. `art-parchment.png` — parchment duo
4. `art-crystal.png` — crystal ensemble
5. `art-glasses.png` — gold group portrait
6. `art-ensemble.png` — green cast montage
7. `art-horizon.png` — cast against the landscape
8. `art-beginnings.png` — English logo / sky landscape
9. `art-swordsmith.png` — blue swordsman poster

`launcher-logo.png` is the supplied English logo from
`Downloads/swordcraft story 3 logo edited away.png` (alpha preserved).
`launcher-village.png` is the previously supplied village illustration from
the HTML preview's asset folder. It is only cropped at display time to fill
the decorative header; the artwork card always fits the complete image.

The existing `boxart-clean.png` and `boxart-original.png` remain in rotation.
The program loads one artwork per opening, never a timed slideshow.
